⚙️ Timetable Setup

Select a school to begin configuring and generating its timetable.

🚀 How it works

  1. Configure teachers and their limits.
  2. Configure subjects and lessons per week.
  3. Configure streams and rooms.
  4. Assign teachers to subjects.
  5. Click Generate Timetable .
  6. The system automatically searches for a valid timetable.

🧭 Recommended Setup Order

Complete the configuration in this order before generating the timetable.

  1. Step 1 — School & academic period
  2. Step 2 — Teachers & workload limits
  3. Step 3 — Subjects & lesson rules
  4. Step 4 — Streams & classrooms
  5. Step 5 — Rooms & facilities
  6. Step 6 — Daily periods & activities
  7. Step 7 — Stream-subject-teacher requirements
  8. Step 8 — Generate, review and resolve conflicts
